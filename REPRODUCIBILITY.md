# Reproducibility notes

This repository is a portfolio presentation of the completed analysis, not a replication package. It contains a PDF report, a CSV transcription of reported regression-table values, and a small plotting script. It does not contain Nielsen or SafeGraph source data, original cleaning/merging code, or model-estimation code.

The CSV was manually transcribed from the research report dated August 24, 2021. Standard errors and coefficients should be checked against that PDF before formal reuse. Figure confidence intervals are visualized as estimate ± 1.96 × reported standard error; they are approximate normal intervals, not a substitute for the original inference procedure.
